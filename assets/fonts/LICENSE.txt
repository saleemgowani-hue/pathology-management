DejaVu Sans / DejaVu Sans Bold
https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/

Bundled here so generated PDFs (utils/pdf_report.py) render the ₹
(Rupee) sign and other non-Latin-1 characters correctly -- reportlab's
built-in base-14 fonts (Helvetica etc.) don't include that glyph.
